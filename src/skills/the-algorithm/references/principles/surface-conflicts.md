# Surface conflicts

**When it applies:** two sources disagree: a test and a claim, a doc and the code, a second opinion and your result, two reviewers.

**Rule:** name both sides and the evidence for each. Do not silently pick one; the disagreement is the signal.

- Re-ask the second opinion with the conflict stated. At most two re-asks on the same conflict; then escalate to the principal.
- Record the resolution, and what decided it, in the run's decisions.

You skipped this when the reply carries one side of a disagreement that the run saw both sides of.
