# Exhaust the design space

**When it applies:** a novel interaction, architectural decision or product choice with no precedent in the codebase, above all one that depends on feel more than logic.

**Rule:** build two or three competing prototypes and compare them side by side before committing to one.

- Make the variants differ in approach, not in detail.
- Keep them throwaway. The comparison is the deliverable, not the code.
- Record which variant won and why in the run's decisions.

You skipped this when the first idea that worked became the design.
